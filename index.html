I’m Ricardo, a software engineer based in Guadalajara, Mexico. I started with mobile apps in 2015 and kept following my curiosity: into financial platforms, enterprise tools, and all the moving parts behind a good product.

I’m happiest when I can connect the dots between what people need and how we build it. Sometimes that’s a React interface. Sometimes it’s a backend service, a difficult bug, or a conversation that gets the team unstuck.

I care about readable code, tests that catch real problems, and sharing what I learn. I don’t have every answer. I do ask questions, get involved, and see things through.
